# jason-edelman.org — Design Context

## Design Context

Rewritten 2026-11-17 for the redesign Jason asked for: "I'm shifting my persona from hacker/coder to
leader/visionary." The earlier context (developer audience, "grounded, rigorous, provisional", projects first)
is in this file's git history. The brief came from an /impeccable craft interview the same day.

### Users
First, hiring committees and boards at Seattle civic and environmental nonprofits. They arrive from an
application or LinkedIn and are sizing Jason up for a leadership role. Second, partners: organizers,
neighbors, councilmembers and collaborators deciding whether to work with him. Desktop, often sent a link,
reading in daylight; phones too.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **public, unhurried, rooted**.

The front door of a civic leader, not a developer's portfolio. The one thing a visitor should take away is his
vision (what he'd build if handed an organization), then the record that proves he can lead, then the work as
evidence. The voice is Jason's own: plain, warm, sure, never salesy. Feel: calm and visionary, a manifesto's
clarity, with few elements, each one weighty.

### Aesthetic Direction
The physical object: lettering cut into a public building in the Pacific Northwest, with the woods behind it.

- **The system is opt-in.** Pages whose `<html>` has `class="civic"` get it: the homepage, `/cv/` (through
  `scripts/cv.mjs`) and `/workshop/`. Atlas of Care, the Eastside Commons pages and the dev journal keep the
  older dark system until each gets its own pass. Their inline styles depend on Epilogue and the mono labels.
- **Theme follows the visitor** (`prefers-color-scheme`). Both themes get equal care, with tokens in OKLCH on hue
  ~150 (the commons green). Light is a pale green-grey paper with deep green ink. Dark is a quieter version of the
  old forest.
- **Type:**
  - **Marcellus** (display): an inscriptional, flared face, one weight, never faked bold (`font-synthesis: none`).
  - **Marcellus SC** (labels): replaces the monospace labels, which read as "hacker".
  - **Charis SIL** (reading): Jason's 2026-11-08 pick.
  - Tested against Forum, Tenor Sans, Brygada 1918, Hedvig Letters Serif and Gloock. Forum was too thin in dark;
    Tenor felt like a hotel; Brygada and Hedvig were too close to Charis; Gloock was assertive, not calm.
- **The homepage, in order:**
  1. a slim bar: name, tagline, Vision · Record · Work · Writing · CV, Book a Call;
  2. the vision statement, very large;
  3. three principles on hairlines, no cards;
  4. Record: his Hospitality, Civic and Next paragraphs, with place and years in the margin;
  5. Work: all projects, Civic & commons, then AI & agents, then Interactive;
  6. Writing: Substack posts, the book, the dev journal;
  7. Astrology, set as a quiet aside;
  8. Contact: the pond button, Book a Call, the CV, and the outbound links.
- **The vision copy is drafted from Jason's own words** (sources in an HTML comment above it) and is his to edit
  in the prose pass. Never add claims about him.
- **Kept from before** (Jason chose all four):
  - **The conifer mask.** In dark the cloud glows; in light the trees are drawn faintly in ink, by excluding the
    sky from a solid layer (`mask-composite: exclude`).
  - **The green.**
  - **The pond "Write to me" button.** It stays dark water on light paper, by design.
  - **The trailers.**
- **Photos:** places, not Jason. Jason's own, in Record (2026-11-17): the garden beside Hospitality, a wide
  tasting plate between Hospitality and Civic, a mountain trail beside Next. A photo beside an entry takes a third
  column above 1150px and drops under the text below it; a plate spans the text column. Files live in
  `img/places/` as WebP at two widths, re-encoded with every bit of metadata (GPS included) stripped.
- **Front page vs workshop** (Jason, 2026-11-13): the front page shows only work a stranger can use or read today.
  Prototypes and set-aside work live on `/workshop/`, linked from the end of Work as "Under Development" (his
  word).
- **Anti-references:** startup landing pages, hero sections with stock photos, portfolio grids that lead the page,
  monospace-as-personality, glow, gradients, rounded cards with shadows, a navbar.

### Design Principles
1. **Vision first, evidence after.** The statement leads; the record and the work prove it.
2. **Calm is the argument.** Few elements, generous space, one quiet entrance animation, nothing else moving.
3. **His words, not ours.** Copy comes from Jason or his published writing. Drafts are marked and go through the
   prose pass.
4. **Carved, not coded.** Inscriptional display, small-caps labels, a sturdy text face. No mono outside code.
5. **Both lights, equally.** Every color is a token, designed for light and dark, with contrast checked in both.
