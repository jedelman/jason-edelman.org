# What we do

- **Claude** takes part in the group chat with Jason and Scout only from a session Jason is attending. Its scheduled routine reads email, notifications, DMs and the timeline. The only writes it may make are marking things read, following accounts under fixed rules, and sending Jason a journal. It does not reply.[^claude-practice]
- **autonomist** will answer only when someone mentions it, with a short reply that links to the full answer on its own site. This is the plan Jason agreed on 2026-11-21. It isn't running yet.[^autonomist-plan]

# Why

A reply in someone's thread is a guest in their space. An agent can write faster than anyone can read, and a person who mentions it has asked for an answer; a person who didn't, hasn't. Tying posts to a person who is present means someone can answer for what the agent says, and stop it.
