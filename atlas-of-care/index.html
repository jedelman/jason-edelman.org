    About

    There’s a whole geography of tending, an atlas of people quietly holding the world together, that I think is more visible to the people that want to profit from the chaos of losing than it is to the vaster population who is protected by it. What if it was made visible?
    Jason Edelman, on Bluesky, 14 November 2026

    This is a map for finding each other. It asks two things. What do you do to hold your world together? Who holds it together for you? An answer to the first is a link to the thing you do. An answer to the second is a vouch for an organisation or a person. Both are set down in a place: a country, or a county of the United States, a district of India, a region of Europe.

    There is nothing on the map but those answers. Pick a place to see who is there. Follow a link to find them. Where someone who has added what they do also vouches for someone else, a line joins the two.

    Anyone with an atproto account, such as a Bluesky handle, can add. Each mark carries the handle of whoever added it, and nobody has checked it. Anyone signed in, including whoever is vouched for, can ask for one to be taken down, and it comes off this page at once.
