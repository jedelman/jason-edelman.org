    There’s a whole geography of tending, an atlas of people quietly holding the world together, that I think is more visible to the people that want to profit from the chaos of losing than it is to the vaster population who is protected by it. What if it was made visible?
    Jason Edelman, on Bluesky, 14 November 2026

    This is a place for finding each other. It asks two things. What do you do to hold your world together? Who holds it together for you? An answer to the first is one sentence in your own words that begins with I: I feed the birds, I cook for my kids, I clean toilets. An answer to the second is a vouch for a sentence someone else has written. And anyone can like a sentence, whether or not they are in a position to vouch for it.

    So nobody here says what another person does. You say your own work, and you can affirm someone else's and be affirmed for yours. The graph draws that and nothing else: a mark for each person who has spoken, an amber line for a vouch, a green one for a like. People who hold each other up drift together.

    Anyone with an atproto account, such as a Bluesky handle, can add. Sign in and the page shows what the people you follow have said. Each sentence carries the handle of whoever wrote it, and nobody has checked it. Anyone signed in can ask for one to be taken down, and it comes off this page at once.
