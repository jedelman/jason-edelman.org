    About

    There’s a whole geography of tending, an atlas of people quietly holding the world together, that I think is more visible to the people that want to profit from the chaos of losing than it is to the vaster population who is protected by it. What if it was made visible?
    Jason Edelman, on Bluesky, 14 November 2026

    This is a map for finding each other. It asks two things. What do you do to hold your world together? Who holds it together for you? An answer to the first is one sentence in your own words that begins with I: I feed the birds, I cook for my kids, I clean toilets. It is set down in a place: a country, or a county of the United States, a district of India, a region of Europe. An answer to the second is a vouch for a sentence someone else has written.

    So nobody here says what another person does. You say your own work, and you can affirm someone else's and be affirmed for yours. There is nothing on the map but that. Pick a place to see who is there. Where someone vouches for what someone else does, a line joins the two.

    Anyone with an atproto account, such as a Bluesky handle, can add. Sign in and the page shows what the people you follow have said, and asks whether you vouch for it. Each sentence carries the handle of whoever wrote it, and nobody has checked it. Anyone signed in can ask for one to be taken down, and it comes off this page at once.
