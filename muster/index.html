    Public and permanent

    Every action is a record in its poster's own atproto repo, as public as a Bluesky post and as permanent. Post only what the organizers already made public. Deleting a post removes it from Muster, but anything written to atproto may already have been copied elsewhere.

    Areas, not addresses

    A place is stored as a square about 5 km across, plus the name you give it. The organizers' own link carries the details. The tally counts areas about 20 by 30 km.

    No member lists

    Nobody signs up to a group here. Reading needs no account and writes nothing anywhere, though the servers it reads from see an IP address, like any website. The open wire finds posters through the relay's index of who has written an action.

    Desks are how collectives vouch

    Anyone can post to the open wire, so check the organizers' link before you go. A desk is a collective's public list of the posters it vouches for and the other desks it trusts. Open a desk to see only what it vouches for. A regional desk is a desk that includes local ones. Nobody runs the whole thing.

    The count is a floor

    A crowd is reported as a range (dozens, hundreds, thousands). The tally adds up the bottom of each range, so the real number is higher.

    What doesn't belong here

    Who is in a group. Plans that aren't public. Anything that could identify someone at risk. Coordination like that belongs somewhere private and encrypted, not on an open network.

    Muster runs in your browser with no server of its own. Actions and desks live in people's own atproto repos; the open wire finds them through the Bluesky relay's collection index. Map data from OpenStreetMap, tiles from OpenFreeMap.
