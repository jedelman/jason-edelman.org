WHRO: Norfolk considers requiring a conditional use permit and public hearing for large data centers (Planning Commission recommended two new categories)

WHRO · origin: City of Norfolk (Planning Commission recommendation and Planning Director's briefing to City Council) · news · 2026-11-20

Data centers under 30,000 square feet or consuming less than 25 megawatts of power would be permitted by default in office, business commerce, industrial and institutional areas. Larger data centers would require a conditional use permit in general industrial, deep waterfront industrial and certain business commerce park districts.

Norfolk's proposed zoning would allow smaller data centers by default and require a public hearing for larger ones; WHRO says the amendment is expected to come before the council in December. WHRO also reports the same 25 MW benchmark appears in Gov. Spanberger's Data Center Accountability Framework.
