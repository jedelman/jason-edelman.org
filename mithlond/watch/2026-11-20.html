Cardinal News: Roanoke City Council asks the General Assembly for a data center moratorium

Cardinal News · news · 2026-11-20

The council’s request, approved as a part of the city’s 2027 legislative agenda this week, asks for the moratorium “until public health and consumer protection standards are adopted to address impacts on energy and water use, drinking water, air and water quality, utility rates, agriculture, parks, historic sites, forests and infrastructure.”

A Virginia city asks for a pause until public health and consumer protection standards are adopted, which bears on who decides how data centers are regulated. Roanoke is in Southwest Virginia, outside Hampton Roads.
