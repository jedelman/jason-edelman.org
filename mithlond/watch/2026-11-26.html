Thursday, November 26, 2026

Virginia Mercury reports that the SCC's evidentiary hearings on the Dominion-NextEra merger ended Tuesday, with a decision due by Jan. 11. In the Mercury's account, Dominion and NextEra offered to send the bill credits first proposed for large load customers, namely data centers, back to residential and other smaller rate classes, and intervenors including the Spanberger administration said the credits were not enough.
