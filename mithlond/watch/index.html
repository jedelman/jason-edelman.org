Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · 1 item

2026-11-24

Cardinal News reports that a Botetourt County circuit judge ordered construction on Google's data center project to stop after ruling that the county's board of supervisors violated Virginia's Freedom of Information Act. The project is in Botetourt County, outside Hampton Roads; it is here for what it says about public process around data center approvals in Virginia.

Saturday, November 21, 2026 · 4 items

2026-11-21

WHRO reports that Norfolk's Planning Director briefed City Council on proposed zoning that would require a conditional use permit and public hearing for data centers over 25 MW or 30,000 square feet, with the amendment expected to come before the council in December. DatacenterDynamics reports that PJM has filed a plan with FERC to speed grid connection of power plants and data centers, with data centers expected to bear the cost, and that the US Senate passed a bill to make data centers pay a larger share of grid costs. Virginia Mercury reports the Data Center Coalition's testimony against Loudoun County's one-year pause.

Friday, November 20, 2026 · 1 item

2026-11-20

Cardinal News reports that Roanoke City Council added a request for a two-year statewide data center moratorium to its 2027 legislative agenda. It is the only new item inside the watch's scope that could be opened today; it concerns Southwest Virginia, not Hampton Roads, and bears on the state-level rules that would govern data centers everywhere.
