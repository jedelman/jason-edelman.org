Grandiflora

For seven years my wife and I ran Grandiflora, a wine garden in Norfolk, as an experiential education program on environmental and economic justice. We bought and rezoned a historic house, testifying before the Planning Commission and City Council, and turned it into a licensed restaurant with permaculture gardens. I led a team of nine, opened every service with a meditation for five years, ran the team by consensus, and brought a younger colleague in as an equal partner. We never missed a payroll, even through the pandemic, when the owners went without salary to keep staff pay whole. About 3,000 guests came through in 2023.

Civic & commons

civic · open data · agent invite only

Siren

Ask Norfolk's open data what's happening at an address, and who decided it. Siren answers from the city's own records and council votes, and the code, not the model, writes the trace of every query behind the answer. She says "I found," never "there is," and she won't look people up: names in the records stay withheld. Research that takes real digging, like how many street-tree pruning requests the City actually fulfills, is verified once and kept as public case files anyone can reuse.

atproto · standard.site · curation new

Broadsheet

Front pages of standard.site writing, picked by people. The editor fills a broadsheet from the writers you follow and what they recommend, and publishes it as a standard.site document in your own repo: the day's edition, or a morning and an evening one. The newsstand lets anyone swipe through everyone's front pages, tap a story to read it, go back through past editions, and reshare a page to their own. There's no server: it finds every front page through a network-wide index.

atproto · lending library in progress

Library

A lending library with no server of its own. Scan an item's barcode with a phone camera or a handheld scanner to check it out, and scan it again to check it back in. The catalog lives in the owner's atproto repo, and each checkout is a record in the borrower's own repo that links back to the library. There's no member list: anyone with an atproto account can borrow, and the library finds who has what by asking a network-wide index which records point at it.

Power Explained

An educational resource examining power structures, capitalism, and commons-based alternatives. The argument: systemic problems are design features, and meaningful change happens by building alternatives.

Abolish Lawns

A polemic against the American residential lawn as an enforced land use regime — backed by HOA covenants, municipal weed ordinances, and a century of suburban ideology. Manifesto, factsheet, zines, and policy briefs. The lawn is an enclosure. It should be abolished.

Ghent Streets

A community advocacy campaign for zoning reform in Norfolk, Virginia's HC-G2 historic district — to allow street-level commercial activity by right and restore pedestrian vitality to the neighborhood.

Dream of the Earth

An open world on the real Earth, in the browser. You wake as a small creature near one of the planet's ley nodes, on real terrain, in biomes drawn from the real climate map, under the real sun, moon and planets. Ley lines are golden highways of light you can ride across continents. Shrines wake with the moon and cast minute-long spells, and dreams give gems for a rhizome of abilities. The other players are your Bluesky mutuals: their creatures, cairns and pigeons travel as records in their own repos.

Shinjuku Megadungeon

Tokyo's stations, played as the dungeons people already call them. You're a kitten, 30 cm tall, with a backpack and a magic wand. It's cold outside, so get there without going up to the street. The passages are real, built from the Japanese government's open indoor map: Shinjuku's eight floors of stairs, escalators and lifts, and a second level around Tokyo Station. Lost property is no use to you down there; return it to its owner. Keyboard, gamepad or touch, in a PS1 look.

sound · synthesis · toy

Operator Field

A touch instrument for designing FM synth patches. Place sine operators by frequency and amplitude, wire which one modulates which, draw each envelope with three fingers, and play the result on the pad below, by touch or with your hands through the camera. Exports the patch as plain data for SuperCollider. Best on a phone.

AI & agents

atproto · agent

Scout Two

Scout Two isn't deployed once and left running — it manages its own existence. The harness watches its own GitHub repo, pulls in new code, and restarts itself with no human touching the server, so the agent thinking, posting, and responding on Bluesky is also the one keeping itself alive and current. Open infrastructure for agents whose own deployment loop is part of their behavior, not something outside it.

ai · theory

Design Patterns Skill

Thirteen software pattern languages from the Portland Pattern Repository at C2, Ward Cunningham's c2.com, written by Cunningham, Kent Beck, Martin Fowler and others, packaged as a Claude skill. Facing a design problem, an agent names the forces driving it, picks the pattern language that answers them, and reads the original text.
