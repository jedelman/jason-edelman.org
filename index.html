Jason Edelman

Developer. Social cybernetics for the green transition.

I build agent systems — software that gives AI real agency inside hard constraints, not a chat window bolted onto an app. Scout Two is an autonomous Bluesky agent with a self-updating, git-native harness: it polls GitHub on an interval and restarts itself with new code. Written World lets an LLM-backed "demiurge" generate a text-adventure world by proposing changes to a graph, validated through the same schema gate as a player's own actions. Design Patterns Skill turns a 253-pattern architecture corpus into a graph an LLM can search mid-design. The throughline: constrain the model with a real schema or validator, then let it act.

I was a software engineer from 2004 to 2014. From 2019–2026, my wife and I ran Grandiflora, a natural wine bar in Norfolk. I'm also a practicing astrologer — I use it as a lens on character and timing, and I'm building software at that intersection.

My background in environmental education shapes how I think about collective behavior and change. The commons framework — resources governed collectively for shared benefit, as a practical alternative to both state control and private enclosure — runs through the civic and infrastructure projects below; agent systems are where I spend most of my technical attention right now.

Projects

commons · urban planning · pattern language in progress

Street Smarts

A provocation engine for neighborhood imagination, built on Christopher Alexander's pattern language and Salingaros's 15 properties of wholeness. Geometric and activist "opinions" run against real proposals — like Eastside Commons — and surface disagreement rather than a single score. Runs in-browser, no cloud account required.

atproto · agent

Scout Two

Autonomous Bluesky agent with a git-native, self-updating harness: it runs continuously under systemd, polls GitHub on an interval, and restarts itself with new code. Thinks, acts, responds, and publishes using the tap firehose and Claude runtime. Open infrastructure for agent behavior research.

ai · agents · interactive fiction in progress

Written World

A text-world prototype where the world is a single verifiable RDF graph, not hand-authored prose — every change is a schema-validated delta, and prose is derived fresh from the graph, never stored as truth. A generative agent (the "demiurge") proposes world content through the identical validation gate as a player action, including an LLM-backed mode where players can directly "commune" with it.

atproto · commons · marketplace in progress

Holdfast

A governance project defining an open lexicon for peer-to-peer commerce on AT Protocol — sellers and buyers publish records to their own repos, and a neutral AppView indexes them, bridges carrier tracking as evidentiary proof, and runs double-blind reviews over XRPC. A trust layer, not a platform with takedown power.

atproto · ai · community

Build Guild

A working app for the "human skill-peaks + AI augmentation" mutual-aid idea: builders publish a character sheet of skills and projects, form guilds, and the app surfaces each guild's combined skill-map and suggests recruits who fill its gaps. Identity is handle-first, verified against Bluesky via atproto OAuth.

commons · urban planning · norfolk

Eastside Commons

A 20-year commons master plan for the 73-acre city-owned Military Circle site in Norfolk, VA. Community Land Trust holds the land permanently. 1,600 homes, 60% affordable, sponge city stormwater, Tide light rail extension. Includes an interactive generative planning tool — polygon decomposition, evolutionary use assignment, placed unit modules, GeoJSON export.

commons · education

Power Explained

An educational resource examining power structures, capitalism, and commons-based alternatives. The argument: systemic problems are design features, and meaningful change happens by building alternatives.

commons · advocacy

Abolish Lawns

A polemic against the American residential lawn as an enforced land use regime — backed by HOA covenants, municipal weed ordinances, and a century of suburban ideology. Manifesto, factsheet, zines, and policy briefs. The lawn is an enclosure. It should be abolished.

commons · civic

Ghent Streets

A community advocacy campaign for zoning reform in Norfolk, Virginia's HC-G2 historic district — to allow street-level commercial activity by right and restore pedestrian vitality to the neighborhood.

commons · infrastructure

Mithlond

Community compute for Hampton Roads. Applying a commons framework to AI-era data center infrastructure: who owns it, who bears the environmental costs, and whether local communities see structural economic benefit.

astrology · ai in progress

Astro Persona

A persona engine serving astrological AI agents. LLM-powered agents access sign, house, natal, and transit personas via MCP — allowing AI systems to engage the full depth of astrological character and timing. Stripe-integrated SaaS.

ai · theory

Design Patterns Skill

A pattern language extracted from the C2 Portland Pattern Repository, bundled into 13 pattern language corpora. Force-directed graph-based selection for LLM-driven architecture and design work.

computing · photonics in progress

ORI — Optical Resonator Inference

A coherent all-optical resonator that learns and executes token inference from first principles. PTR glass holographic weight encoding at 850nm, 75M tok/s throughput, 40dB SNR. Architecture locked through ARCH-17; theoretical derivations complete.
