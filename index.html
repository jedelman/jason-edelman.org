Jason Edelman

Developer. Social cybernetics for the green transition.

I build agent systems — software that gives AI real agency inside hard constraints, not a chat window bolted onto an app. Siren answers questions about Norfolk from the city's own open data, and the code, not the model, writes the record of how she found each answer. Scout Two is an autonomous Bluesky agent with a self-updating, git-native harness: it polls GitHub on an interval and restarts itself with new code. Written World lets an LLM-backed "demiurge" generate a text-adventure world by proposing changes to a graph, validated through the same schema gate as a player's own actions. Design Patterns Skill gives an agent thirteen software pattern languages from C2's Portland Pattern Repository, picked by the forces driving the design problem in front of it. The throughline: constrain the model with a real schema or validator, then let it act.

I was a software engineer from 2004 to 2014. From 2019–2026, my wife and I ran Grandiflora, a natural wine bar in Norfolk. I'm also a practicing astrologer — I use it as a lens on character and timing, and I'm building software at that intersection.

My background in environmental education shapes how I think about collective behavior and change. The commons framework — resources governed collectively for shared benefit, as a practical alternative to both state control and private enclosure — runs through the civic and infrastructure projects below; agent systems are where I spend most of my technical attention right now.

Projects

civic · open data · agent invite only

Siren

Ask Norfolk's open data what's happening at an address, and who decided it. Siren answers from the city's own records and council votes, and the code, not the model, writes the trace of every query behind the answer. She says "I found," never "there is," and she won't look people up: names in the records stay withheld. Research that takes real digging, like how many street-tree pruning requests the City actually fulfills, is verified once and kept as public case files anyone can reuse.

sound · supercollider · instrument in progress

Goetia

A modular synth and performance engine for SuperCollider, played from a desk of controllers: Launchpad, Launch Control XL, Mixtrack, Seaboard. Its FM patches come from Operator Field, and its words come from the Ars Goetia: patches are spirits kept in a grimoire, the running engine is the circle, and you conjure it and give it license to depart. With no hardware, a browser stands in for the whole desk, or your hands do, through the camera: the right hand signs, the left holds the world.

atproto · lending library in progress

Library

A lending library with no server of its own. Scan an item's barcode with a phone camera or a handheld scanner to check it out, and scan it again to check it back in. The catalog lives in the owner's atproto repo, and each checkout is a record in the borrower's own repo that links back to the library. There's no member list: anyone with an atproto account can borrow, and the library finds who has what by asking a network-wide index which records point at it.

commons · urban planning · pattern language in progress

Street Smarts

Christopher Alexander argued a place's "aliveness" can't be measured — Street Smarts takes that seriously and builds a tool anyway. It runs a real proposal, like Eastside Commons, through geometric and activist opinions designed to disagree, giving a coalition something to argue with instead of a black-box score. Fifty years of pattern-language theory, made usable by the people zoning law usually leaves out of the room.

atproto · agent

Scout Two

Scout Two isn't deployed once and left running — it manages its own existence. The harness watches its own GitHub repo, pulls in new code, and restarts itself with no human touching the server, so the agent thinking, posting, and responding on Bluesky is also the one keeping itself alive and current. Open infrastructure for agents whose own deployment loop is part of their behavior, not something outside it.

ai · agents · interactive fiction in progress

Written World

Text worlds a model generates have lacked what 3D world models take for granted: a world state that's verifiable, not just prose. In Written World, an LLM can be handed real creative license — inventing objects, relations, even kinds of interaction the author never wrote — because every idea it proposes has to clear the identical validation gate as a player's own move before it becomes real. The AI gets genuine agency precisely because nothing it does is trusted by default.

atproto · commons · marketplace in progress

Holdfast

A marketplace's trust — reviews, reputation, dispute history — normally lives locked inside that company's database, gone the moment you leave the platform. Holdfast puts it on the open, public AT Protocol firehose instead: portable, permanent, and readable by any provider, with no company holding the money, the listings, or the power to de-list you. A commons argument aimed at Amazon and eBay's real asset — not the marketplace, but the trust data itself.

atproto · ai · community

Build Guild

Build Guild turns a single Bluesky thread's pitch — team up with people whose skills cover your gaps, "100% like an MMORPG guild" — into a real, working app, not just an idea people agreed with and forgot. It skips building yet another identity system: a builder's Bluesky handle is their verified identity, checked live against the public network, with login keys that live entirely on-device and never touch the server.

commons · urban planning · norfolk

Eastside Commons

A 20-year commons master plan for the 73-acre city-owned Military Circle site in Norfolk, VA. Community Land Trust holds the land permanently. 1,600 homes, 60% affordable, sponge city stormwater, Tide light rail extension. Includes an interactive generative planning tool — polygon decomposition, evolutionary use assignment, placed unit modules, GeoJSON export.

commons · education

Power Explained

An educational resource examining power structures, capitalism, and commons-based alternatives. The argument: systemic problems are design features, and meaningful change happens by building alternatives.

commons · advocacy

Abolish Lawns

A polemic against the American residential lawn as an enforced land use regime — backed by HOA covenants, municipal weed ordinances, and a century of suburban ideology. Manifesto, factsheet, zines, and policy briefs. The lawn is an enclosure. It should be abolished.

commons · civic

Ghent Streets

A community advocacy campaign for zoning reform in Norfolk, Virginia's HC-G2 historic district — to allow street-level commercial activity by right and restore pedestrian vitality to the neighborhood.

sound · synthesis · toy

Operator Field

A touch instrument for designing FM synth patches. Place sine operators by frequency and amplitude, wire which one modulates which, draw each envelope with three fingers, and play the result on the pad below, by touch or with your hands through the camera. Exports the patch as plain data for SuperCollider. Best on a phone.

ai · theory

Design Patterns Skill

Thirteen software pattern languages from the Portland Pattern Repository at C2, Ward Cunningham's c2.com, written by Cunningham, Kent Beck, Martin Fowler and others, packaged as a Claude skill. Facing a design problem, an agent names the forces driving it, picks the pattern language that answers them, and reads the original text.

computing · photonics in progress

ORI — Optical Resonator Inference

A coherent all-optical resonator that learns and executes token inference from first principles. PTR glass holographic weight encoding at 850nm, 75M tok/s throughput, 40dB SNR. Architecture locked through ARCH-17; theoretical derivations complete.
