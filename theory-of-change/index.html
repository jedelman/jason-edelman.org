Build and Defend the Commons

Theory of change

← Home

The most significant parts of our lives are common. Work, play, culture, relationships, even our health, are all the results of collective efforts. The real sources of joy, power, and well-being in our lives are things that we build through our choice and participation.

We make decisions as if we are isolated from each other because the feeling of isolation is what we have in common. Isolation has many mechanisms - financial, physical, and social - but the patterns of affect and behavior that reinforce our belief in isolation, and the personal philosophy of individualism that results, are the actual common that we build and defend every day. In other words, we work hard to be isolated.

It’s easy to see why when you try to imagine an alternative. Note the automatic objections, the fears and anxieties and even trauma, that simply imagining the end of our isolation brings. This is a major hurdle to overcome, and that is where my work lies. But none of us can do it alone - that’s how we ended up here in the first place. That’s why I’m inviting you to do the work with me.

Try this: what if we were to take that energy and build something else?
