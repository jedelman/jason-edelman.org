Field model: each pattern deposits pressure across the site. A location matters because multiple patterns want it simultaneously — not because one pattern owns it.

Max passes caps the run. Saturate ε: if mean field change drops below ε, the solver stops early. With patterns in tension (P104 vs P60, P115 vs P122), the field may oscillate and never saturate — that's correct behaviour.

Resolution: grid cell size in feet. 10ft = fine, 25ft = standard, 50ft = fast. Patterns use real architectural distances.

Threshold: minimum combined pressure to trace a field line.

Line weight: proportional to combined pressure at that point. Color: dominant pattern mix.
