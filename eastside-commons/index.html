The city of Norfolk bought Military Circle Mall for $10. The land is ours — the Eastside's. A Community Land Trust holds it in perpetuity. The appreciation stays in the commons. No developer buys in, extracts for 50 years, and leaves.

This site plan derives all geometry from the actual GIS parcel data — 19 confirmed EDA-owned parcels, real coordinates, real ownership from the Norfolk FY26 assessment roll. District zones emerge from the parcels themselves as zones of influence: each district radiates from its anchor parcels, and boundaries form at local equilibria, not at drawn lines.
