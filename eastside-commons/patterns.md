# Eastside Commons · Pattern Language Reference

Patterns drawn from Christopher Alexander, Sara Ishikawa, Murray Silverstein et al.,  
*A Pattern Language* (1977). Full text at [patternlanguageindex.com](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com).

These 53 patterns form the generative grammar of the Eastside Commons constraint solver.  
Each scale constrains the next. The solver recurses until every zone satisfies  
the pattern obligations of its neighbors.

---

## Scale 1 — Site / District
*Zone placement, density, exposure, movement spine*

| # | Pattern | Geometric role |
|---|---------|---------------|
| [P21](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/four-story-limit) | Four-Story Limit | Max height envelope. Override invoked: transit corridor condition. |
| [P29](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/density-rings) | Density Rings | Density gradient from spine outward. Feeds zone FAR targets. |
| [P30](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/activity-nodes) | Activity Nodes | Anchor points that attract movement. Commercial zones must cluster around them. |
| [P31](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/promenade) | Promenade | The spine. Every point on site within 10-min walk. Main attractor at each end. |
| [P36](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/degrees-of-publicness) | Degrees of Publicness | Quiet backwater vs busy street. Assigns exposure level to each zone. |
| [P53](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/main-gateways) | Main Gateways | Entry markers at every boundary crossing. Change of light, level, surface. |
| [P62](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/high-places) | High Places | Deliberate exception to P21. Vista points. The Eastside Market tower or civic marker. |

## Scale 2 — Block / Cluster
*Zone relationships, open space obligations, path network*

| # | Pattern | Geometric role |
|---|---------|---------------|
| [P37](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/house-cluster) | House Cluster | 8–12 households around common land. Base residential unit. Paths pass through openly. |
| [P51](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/green-streets) | Green Streets | Car-permeable but pedestrian-priority. No curb separation. Grass between paving stones. |
| [P52](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/network-of-paths-and-cars) | Network of Paths and Cars | Path network topology. Loops, not grids. Cars always secondary. |
| [P59](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/quiet-backs) | Quiet Backs | Rear alley network connecting gardens. Not a shortcut for through traffic. |
| [P60](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/accessible-green) | Accessible Green | Green within 3-min walk of every home. Hard constraint on zone placement. |
| [P61](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/small-public-squares) | Small Public Squares | 45–70ft wide. Area = 150–300 × expected pedestrian count (P123). |
| [P67](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/common-land) | Common Land | Shared open space for each cluster. South-facing. Connects to P68. |
| [P68](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/connected-play) | Connected Play | Continuous child-safe path through green zones. ≥64 households connected without crossing traffic. |
| [P69](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/public-outdoor-room) | Public Outdoor Room | Covered outdoor gathering space. Beside main path. Within view of homes and workshops. |
| [P71](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/still-water) | Still Water | Pool or bioswale. South-facing. Gradually deepening. Our sponge park. |
| [P99](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/main-building) | Main Building | Tallest, most prominent = soul of the complex. Every group needs one. |

## Scale 3 — Building Group
*How buildings arrange relative to each other and to open space*

| # | Pattern | Geometric role |
|---|---------|---------------|
| [P95](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/building-complex) | Building Complex | Break mass into smaller units. Piecemeal. 25–30ft frontage. One at a time. |
| [P96](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/number-of-stories) | Number of Stories | Height from FAR + surroundings. Max 50% site coverage. Top floor = ¾ area of lower. |
| [P97](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/shielded-parking) | Shielded Parking | Parking hidden from paths. Peripheral. Max ~9% of site. |
| [P98](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/circulation-realms) | Circulation Realms | Named zones navigable by description. Gateway hierarchy. |
| [P100](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/pedestrian-street) | Pedestrian Street | Movement spine. Small enough to feel full. Shops and entrances along edge. |
| [P101](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/building-thoroughfare) | Building Thoroughfare | Interior street when density forces it. Must connect to outside at both ends. |
| [P102](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/family-of-entrances) | Family of Entrances | All entrances visible from each other. Family resemblance. No hidden doors. |
| [P103](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/small-parking-lots) | Small Parking Lots | Scattered small lots, not one large one. Hidden behind buildings. |
| [P104](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/site-repair) | Site Repair | Build on worst land. Preserve best. Site and buildings as single living ecosystem. |
| [P105](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/south-facing-outdoors) | South Facing Outdoors | Buildings to north of their outdoor spaces. Never shade the sun side. |
| [P106](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/positive-outdoor-space) | Positive Outdoor Space | Outdoor space has definite shape — not leftover between buildings. |
| [P107](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/wings-of-light) | Wings of Light | Building breaks into narrow wings. Wings ≤25ft wide. Shapes outdoor space between. |
| [P108](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/connected-buildings) | Connected Buildings | No setbacks between buildings. New buildings continue older ones. Arcades where walls can't meet. |
| [P109](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/long-thin-house) | Long Thin House | Narrow plan: 8–25ft. The 25×125 parcel is this pattern. Shapes outdoor space by its perimeter. |
| [P114](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/hierarchy-of-open-space) | Hierarchy of Open Space | Every space looks into a smaller AND a larger space. No dead-end enclosures. |

## Scale 4 — Building Form
*Geometry derived from pattern obligations*

| # | Pattern | Geometric role |
|---|---------|---------------|
| [P110](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/main-entrance) | Main Entrance | Visible from all lines of approach. Bold shape. Stands out. |
| [P111](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/half-hidden-garden) | Half-Hidden Garden | Lot roughly square or wide-shallow. Garden partially visible from street. |
| [P112](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/entrance-transition) | Entrance Transition | Change of light, sound, direction, surface, level between street and door. |
| [P115](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/courtyards-which-live) | Courtyards Which Live | Courtyard must have view out to larger space. ≥2 doors crossing it. Porch at one edge. |
| [P116](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/cascade-of-roofs) | Cascade of Roofs | Roofs step down toward wings. Highest at center. Building reads as roof system first. |
| [P119](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/arcades) | Arcades | Covered walkway connecting buildings along paths. Low ceiling. Thick columns to lean on. |
| [P121](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/path-shape) | Path Shape | Path bulges in middle, narrows at ends. Creates a place, not just a route. |
| [P122](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/building-fronts) | Building Fronts | No setbacks from paths. Build right to edge. Slightly uneven angles for character. |
| [P123](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/pedestrian-density) | Pedestrian Density | Area of any gathering space = 150–300 × expected people (P). Sizes squares correctly. |
| [P124](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/activity-pockets) | Activity Pockets | Small partly-enclosed areas at edges of open space. Jut forward into the space. |
| [P127](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/intimacy-gradient) | Intimacy Gradient | Public → private sequence from entrance inward. Never private rooms at front. |
| [P129](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/common-areas-at-the-heart) | Common Areas at the Heart | Shared space at center of gravity of all paths through a building. |
| [P160](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/building-edge) | Building Edge | Edge is a zone with depth. Crenelated. Places to stop, sit, lean. Not a line. |
| [P163](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/outdoor-room) | Outdoor Room | Columns + partial roof = outdoor space that feels like a room. Open to sky. |
| [P165](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/opening-to-the-street) | Opening to the Street | Commercial space fully opens to path. Activity straddles the path itself. |

## Scale 5 — Street Edge
*Ground-floor activation rules*

| # | Pattern | Geometric role |
|---|---------|---------------|
| [P32](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/shopping-street) | Shopping Street | Short pedestrian street at right angles to arterial. Parking behind, not in front. |
| [P46](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/market-of-many-shops) | Market of Many Shops | Roof + columns + open stalls. Aisles 6–12ft wide. Stalls as small as 6×9ft. Our Eastside Market. |
| [P79](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/your-own-home) | Your Own Home | Each unit modifiable by resident. Lot wide-shallow, not narrow-deep. Garden access. |
| [P87](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/individually-owned-shops) | Individually Owned Shops | Each shop identifiable unit. Opens to street. Goods visible and available. |
| [P88](https://www.patternlanguageindex.com/patterns/street-cafe) | Street Cafe | Tables extend into path. Watches the world go by. Opens fully to street (P165). |

## Eastside Commons instantiation notes

| Pattern | How Eastside Commons instantiates it |
|---------|--------------------------------------|
| P21 | 6 stories (override: I-264/I-64 transit nexus condition, per P62 exception) |
| P29 | Density rings: spine corridor highest, sponge park edge lowest |
| P31 | Commons spine = promenade. Eastside Market at south end, civic cluster at north |
| P46 | Eastside Market (Phase 0) — food hall, 40–60 vendors, 6×9ft stalls |
| P51 | All interior streets: grass between paving, no curbs, cars guests |
| P60 | 25% site as green/open. Every residential zone within 600ft of green |
| P67 | Courtyard common land in each 5-over-1 block. South-facing |
| P71 | Sponge Park: 3.1ac bioswale + wetland basin + research pavilion |
| P79 | CLT condos at cost basis. Each unit modifiable. Lot wide-shallow |
| P87 | Ground floor commercial in every 5-over-1. Each bay identifiable |
| P95 | 25×125 parcels. One building at a time. 25ft frontage. Piecemeal |
| P104 | Mall surface parking = worst land. Build there. Preserve Sponge Park |
| P109 | The 25×125 parcel *is* the Long Thin House. 25ft wide = the pattern |
| P115 | Every 5-over-1 courtyard: view to larger green space, 2 doors crossing |
| P122 | Buildings at edge of 25ft parcels. No setbacks on spine |
